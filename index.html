Section 01

The moment that's worth €42,800.

María González Ruiz lives in Madrid. She's 41, has banked with Santander for 9 years, and her €187,400 mortgage matures in exactly 90 days. Her current rate — 3.85%, variable, tied to EURIBOR — is about to renegotiate. NPS 8. Lifetime value at risk: €42,800.

The signals are already there. In the last twelve days, she's opened Santander's mobile app fourteen times, dwelled an average of four minutes on the rate-switching page on Santander.es, and read the rate-comparison article in the mortgage section twice. The race isn't whether someone will talk to her about her mortgage. It's who reaches her with the right tone, at the right moment.

Section 02 · The setup

Signals from everywhere converge into one María.

Adobe Experience Platform unifies thirteen signals across three layers. Quantitative: what she's doing — clicks, dwell, transactions. Qualitative: what she's feeling — modeled mindset, likely barriers, life events. Modeled: what's likely to happen — churn propensity, expected lift, channel responsiveness.

Most retention programmes work with quantitative signals only. Adding the qualitative layer is what makes the next move feel like a response — not a campaign.

Section 03 · The setup

NBX isn't one model. It's a four-layer stack.

Adobe Journey Optimizer runs a stack of decisions in sequence. Deterministic rules check eligibility. Probabilistic models score propensity. A contextual bandit picks the arc with highest expected lift. Guardrails enforce compliance and brand on every output.

For María, the stack selects a three-phase arc: Reassurance (T-90 to T-60) → Comparison (T-60 to T-30) → Decision (T-30 to T-15). Same customer, three tones, three moments.

1

Eligibility — deterministic

Hard rules: maturity window, product holdings, opt-ins, exclusion lists.

2

Propensity — probabilistic

Churn likelihood, channel response, time-of-day responsiveness.

3

Arm selection — contextual bandit

Pick the experience arc with highest expected lift for this mindset.

4

Guardrails — governance

Compliance disclosures, brand checks, frequency caps, regulatory tags.

💡
Why this matters
In banking, every decision must be explainable. Rules give regulators the "why qualified." Propensity gives marketing the lift. Guardrails keep both within brand and compliance. One layer alone is never enough.
Section 04 · The production

NBX writes the brief. CSC produces the content.

The decision NBX just made becomes a structured brief into Adobe GenStudio. Four audience variants × three channels × two stages — roughly 190 individual content pieces, each one specified, branded, and compliance-tagged before anything is created.

At human-only velocity, producing 190 governed, multi-language assets takes weeks. With CSC, it takes hours.

Section 06 · The production

Who owns the risk when AI writes? Everyone — gated.

Every variant passes through four gates before publishing. Auto brand check using a model fine-tuned on Santander's tone. Compliance review routing for rate-bearing copy. Human marketing approval, with rejections feeding back into the model. And a full audit trail — model version, prompt, every edit, every approver, timestamped.

Section 08 · The arc plays out

T-45 · The mindset shifts. So does the arc.

In the last 7 days, María opened the comparison email, clicked through to Santander's rate-options page, and dwelled 4 minutes on the rate-switching content. She also opened the mortgage section of the app three times. NBX reads the signals: mindset is no longer Reassurance. It's Comparison — verging on Decision.

The next move escalates from automated touchpoints to her Relationship Manager. CSC generates a one-page personalized briefing for the RM — barriers, likely opportunity, recommended talking points — in real time.

Section 10 · The arc plays out

T-12 · She signs. Three channels go silent.

Three days before maturity, María signs Tipo Fijo 3.15% in branch. The signature triggers an instant suppression cascade — every queued touchpoint is cancelled. No "congratulations on your renewal" email two days later. No app push for an offer already accepted.

The next arc — family-home upgrade, detected from her T-90 search intent — quietly opens.
